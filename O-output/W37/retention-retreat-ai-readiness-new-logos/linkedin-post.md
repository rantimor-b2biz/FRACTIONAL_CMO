## POST CAPTION

Your board just approved an "AI-led growth" plan that will make you less AI-ready.

They don't know it yet. Neither do most CMOs.

Here's the move happening right now: retention budget gets cut, acquisition spend goes up, and it all gets labeled AI-led growth. The new-logo chart looks great in the room.

Gartner's 2026 CMO Spend survey shows loyalty and retention has dropped to under 15% of media spend, a 29% cut, as budgets shift to acquisition.

The problem is what that same Gartner data also shows.

The companies putting MORE budget into retention are the ones that are MORE AI-mature. Not less.

That's not a coincidence.

AI runs on customer data. Usage patterns. Renewal history. Expansion signals. Churn behavior.

None of that comes from a new logo. It comes from the retention and customer success motion you just defunded to look AI-forward.

So you cut the exact function that generates the fuel your AI depends on, and call it acceleration.

For Israeli B2B startups this hits harder, because land-and-expand was always the real growth engine. The board just asked you to dismantle it for a metric that photographs well in a deck.

Full breakdown in the first comment.

If your board is pushing for AI-led growth, what got cut to pay for it?
