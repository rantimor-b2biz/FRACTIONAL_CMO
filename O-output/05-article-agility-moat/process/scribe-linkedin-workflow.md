### Linkedin Strategy Skill Structure (From Skill File)

**Hook:** Contrarian/bold statement (1-2 sentences)
- Opens with perspective that challenges conventional wisdom
- Makes reader stop scrolling
- Sets up the insight that follows

**Context:** Why this matters (2-3 sentences)
- Why should founders care about this?
- What's at stake?
- What's the business impact?

**Insight:** Strategic take (3-4 sentences)
- The core teaching from the article
- Specific examples, not generic advice
- Backed by experience

**Application:** What to do (2-3 sentences)
- Actionable takeaway
- What should the founder do differently?
- Something they can implement this week

**CTA:** Question for engagement (1 sentence)
- Drives conversation in comments
- Authentic, not manipulative

**Tone Rules:**
- Executive voice (speaks to CEOs/founders)
- Direct and crisp (no fluff)
- Slightly contrarian (takes a position)
- Short sentences (most under 15 words)
- No buzzwords or corporate jargon
- No em dashes (or max 1 in LinkedIn)
- Specific examples throughout

**Length:** 150-300 words (optimal for LinkedIn engagement)

**Key Points:**

- For founders with limited runway, agility IS the insurance

### **Alternative Angle 2: Agility Creates Founder Advantage (Not Company Advantage)**

**The Insight:** Founders with agile organizations attract better people, close better deals, raise easier, build faster. Agility compounds founder advantage specifically.

**Why It Resonates:** Israeli founders know they compete with deeper-pocketed global competitors. Agility is the unfair advantage they actually have.

- Customers trust founders who move fast (not competitors with committees)

### **Alternative Angle 3: Agility as Anti-Fragility**

**Why It Resonates:** Israeli entrepreneurship literally built itself through shocks. This is the deeper pattern.

**Key Points:**
- Every crisis = 100 small decisions that compound advantage
- Rigid competitors break under pressure
- Agile organizations get faster (and better) through pressure
