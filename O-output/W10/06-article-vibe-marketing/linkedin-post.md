## POST CAPTION

Vibe coding has a marketing equivalent.
It's called vibe marketing. And it's costing B2B startups deals.

Vibe marketing = producing content, campaigns, and GTM motion before the strategic foundation is clear.

It looks like execution.
Your board sees activity.
Your pipeline doesn't feel it.

Signs you're doing it:
→ Positioning changes after every important meeting
→ AI writes the post before you decide what commercial job it has to do
→ Your sales team doesn't use your marketing content

In consumer, volume covers imprecision.
For B2B tech startups, it compounds. Sales cycles don't forgive vague positioning.

The fix is not to slow down. It's to sequence correctly.
Foundation first. Then AI amplifies what actually works.

Full breakdown in first comment.

Are you running on strategy or on vibes?
