# Draft v1 — 2026-07-02

Your CRM says the funnel is healthy.

Your buyer already picked a vendor before your CRM knew they existed.

Most Israeli B2B founders are still reporting MQLs and funnel conversion like it's 2019. Meanwhile the funnel they're tracking covers a shrinking minority of the actual buying journey.

Here's why.

According to G2's Answer Economy report (Dec 2025), 51% of B2B software buyers now start their research in an AI chatbot, not a search engine.

According to 6sense, 73% of the B2B buying journey happens anonymously before a buyer ever contacts a vendor. 83% fully define their requirements before speaking to sales.

And here's the part that should worry every GTM leader: G2 found 69% of buyers chose a different vendor than they originally planned, based on what an AI chatbot told them. One-third bought from a vendor they'd never even heard of.

That decision happened in a conversation you will never see. By the time it hits your CRM as a "lead," the shortlist is already set.

And this is happening while marketing budgets flatline at 7.7% of revenue (Gartner, 2025) - so you can't out-spend the problem either.

So building a better lead-scoring model doesn't fix this. It just measures the funnel more precisely after the decision has already been made upstream.

This isn't a demand-gen problem. It's a positioning problem wearing a demand-gen costume.

If an AI chatbot doesn't understand what you do or who you do it for, it won't recommend you - no matter how good your MQL dashboard looks.

Have you actually checked what ChatGPT or Perplexity says about your company when a prospect asks?
