## POST CAPTION

Everyone says the CMO is dead.

They're wrong. It's not dead. It's being carved up and handed out one piece at a time.

In two weeks, three separate studies described the same thing from three angles.

Pricing moves to the Chief Commercial Officer.
Data moves to the Chief Data Officer.
Execution moves to AI agents.

What's left for marketing? The brand deck.

Everyone calls this modernization. I think it's why positioning keeps failing.

Here's the part the AI hype misses: AI collapses the cost of execution. It does not collapse the need for judgment about what to execute.

Gartner's 2026 CMO Spend Survey found CMOs pouring 15.3% of budget into AI, while only 30% call their AI readiness mature.

So you get five departments generating content five times faster, from five different assumptions about who the customer even is.

You can split tasks across five people.

You cannot split judgment.

For a 20 to 200 person startup, the fix isn't more chiefs. It's fewer owners with real authority, and AI doing the work underneath them, not around them.

Full breakdown in the first comment.

If you handed your one-liner to three people on your team right now, would you get one answer or three?
