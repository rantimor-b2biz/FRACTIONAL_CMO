## Post (copy-paste to LinkedIn)

Everyone assumed 2026 budgets would grow to match the AI ambition. They didn't.

Marketing budgets sat at 7.8% of company revenue this year, up from 7.7% in 2025 (Marketing Week, on the Gartner 2026 CMO Spend Survey).

That's flat for the second year running.

So the smartest CMOs did something quiet.

They stopped cutting evenly.

Instead of trimming every program by 10%, they pulled money out of demand gen entirely and moved it into brand and positioning.

Why that direction?

Because demand gen is rented. Stop paying, and the pipeline stops the same week. It never compounds.

Positioning is the one line item that keeps paying off in every deal, every quarter, with zero incremental spend.

Here's the part that stings for Israeli founders.

The reflex the moment a round closes is to buy pipeline before fixing the message.

The 2026 data says that's backwards.

Full breakdown in the first comment.

If your budget is flat next year, where does the first dollar go: demand gen or positioning?
