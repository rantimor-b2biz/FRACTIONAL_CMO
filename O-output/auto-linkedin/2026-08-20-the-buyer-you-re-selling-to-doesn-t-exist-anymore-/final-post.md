Your sales deck was built for a buyer who no longer exists.

Not as an insult. As a fact.

64% of B2B buyers at manager level and above are now Millennials or Gen Z (Forrester, Buyers' Journey Survey 2025).

They didn't inherit the old enterprise buying committee. They rebuilt it: self-guided, skeptical of outreach, allergic to being sold to.

Most founders are still optimizing for the wrong person.

Nurture sequences paced for someone who reads a whitepaper and waits for a call.

Rep enablement that assumes the sales conversation is where the buyer decides.

Both assumptions are dead.

Here's the part that should worry you: nearly half of first-time buyers already have a preferred vendor in mind before your GTM motion officially begins.

The decision happens in silence, before your SDR sends a single email.

More AI personalization won't fix this. It just produces faster, better-written generic outreach that still gets filtered out.

The real battleground moved. It's no longer the sales call. It's whatever a self-guided buyer finds when they research you with zero human interaction.

If your positioning can't do the convincing alone, no amount of rep skill recovers the deal.
