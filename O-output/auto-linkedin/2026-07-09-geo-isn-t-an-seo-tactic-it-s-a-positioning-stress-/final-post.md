Everyone's asking me for a GEO strategy.

Most of them are trying to solve the wrong problem.

GEO (getting cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini instead of ranked by Google) is being sold as the next SEO.

Structure your data. Increase your fact density. Write content models can quote.

None of it is wrong. It's just beside the point.

Here's what nobody selling GEO packages wants to say:

A search engine gives you ten blue links. A weak positioning can still show up in the list and let a human sort out the difference.

An LLM gives you one synthesized answer.

There's no list to hide in.

You either own a sharp, distinct claim in the sources the model pulled from, or you get averaged into the generic middle and never appear at all.

Structured vagueness is still vagueness. The model just processes it faster.

And this is already happening. Per Cognism's 2026 B2B trends report (Nov 26, 2025), Google's search share just dropped below 90% for the first time in a decade, and their own organic sessions are down 33.6% year-over-year.

The buying journey is moving off your site, into an answer you don't control.

GEO doesn't reward better content hygiene.

It exposes the companies that never did the positioning work.
