# LinkedIn Post — 2026-07-02 — The Dark Funnel Is Now the Whole Funnel: Your Pipeline Metrics Are Measuring a Buying Journey That No Longer Exists

> Pillar: Demand Generation — beyond leads, measuring what matters · Gatekeeper: REVISED

## Post (copy-paste to LinkedIn)

Your CRM says the funnel is healthy.

Your buyer already picked a vendor before your CRM knew they existed.

Most Israeli B2B founders are still reporting MQLs and funnel conversion like it's 2019. But the funnel they're tracking now covers a shrinking minority of the actual buying journey.

Here's why.

G2's Answer Economy report (Dec 2025) found 51% of B2B software buyers now start their research in an AI chatbot, not a search engine.

6sense found 73% of the buying journey happens anonymously, before a buyer ever contacts a vendor. 83% fully define their requirements before speaking to sales.

And here's the part that should worry every GTM leader:

G2 found 69% of buyers chose a different vendor than they originally planned, based on what an AI chatbot told them. One-third bought from a vendor they'd never heard of before.

That decision happened in a conversation you will never see.

By the time it hits your CRM as a "lead," the shortlist is already set.

And this is happening while marketing budgets sit flat at 7.7% of revenue (Gartner, 2025). You can't out-spend the problem either.

So building a better lead-scoring model doesn't fix this. It just measures the funnel more precisely after the decision was already made upstream.

This isn't a demand-gen problem. It's a positioning problem wearing a demand-gen costume.

If an AI chatbot doesn't understand what you do or who you do it for, it won't recommend you. No matter how good your MQL dashboard looks.

When did you last check what ChatGPT or Perplexity actually says about your company when a prospect asks?
