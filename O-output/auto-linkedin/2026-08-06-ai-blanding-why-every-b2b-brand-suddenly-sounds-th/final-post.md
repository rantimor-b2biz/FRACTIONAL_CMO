## Post (copy-paste to LinkedIn)

Every B2B homepage suddenly reads like it was written by the same person.

It basically was. Just not a person.

The easy story is that AI tools are flooding the market with generic content. That's not quite right.

AI doesn't create the sameness. It industrializes whatever you feed it.

If your positioning was already vague, AI just mass-produces that vagueness. Faster and cheaper than any junior marketer ever could.

Here's the tell: in Marketing Week's State of B2B Marketing 2025, marketers are now openly naming "the risk of blanding" as a live problem, right while they scale AI content.

Not a future forecast. A problem they're watching happen.

I've handed the same AI model two briefs from two founders in the same category.

One brief was generic. The output sounded like forty competitors.

The other brief had a specific buyer, a specific mechanism, and a number. The output sounded like nobody else.

Same tool. Different input.

The fix isn't less AI. It's sequencing: positioning is the actual prompt, and it has to exist before the pipeline goes live.
