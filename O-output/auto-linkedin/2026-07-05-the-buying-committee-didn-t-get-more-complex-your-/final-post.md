Every B2B report this month says buying committees exploded to 16 people.

The fix everyone's selling is wrong.

More personalization. More content variants. More ABM tooling to reach all sixteen stakeholders with a message tailored to each.

That's treating a symptom.

A 16-person buying committee isn't a distribution problem. It's a positioning stress test.

Here's what nobody says out loud:

At least 10 of those 16 people will never talk to your sales team.

They hear about you secondhand. A Slack message. A hallway conversation. A summary slide someone else built.

Your champion has to explain your product to finance, legal, and IT in a meeting you're not in.

That's the moment your positioning holds or collapses.

Not the pitch. Not the deck. The retell.

And it matters more now than ever. 81% of B2B buyers pick their vendor before they ever talk to sales (6Sense, cited via Effiqs).

So if your story only works when you're the one telling it, you've already lost most of the room.

More content variants don't fix that. They just spread the confusion faster.
