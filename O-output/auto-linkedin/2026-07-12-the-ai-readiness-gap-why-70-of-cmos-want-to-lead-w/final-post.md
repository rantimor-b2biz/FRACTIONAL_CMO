## Post (copy-paste to LinkedIn)

70% of CMOs want to lead with AI in 2026.

Only 30% actually can.

Every board reads that Gartner stat and reaches for the same lever: more AI budget, an AI hire, a new stack.

Wrong lever.

Here's what the same survey quietly proves. AI-mature companies allocate 21.3% of budget to AI. The average CMO sits at 15.3%. Looks like a spend gap.

But total marketing budgets are flat (7.7% to 7.8% of revenue). Nobody is buying their way in with fresh money.

The mature companies reallocate what they have, because their foundation already makes that reallocation productive.

Readiness comes first. The spend follows.

"We're not AI-ready" almost always means "we were never marketing-ready."

Documented ICP. Positioning that survives a sales call. Clean customer data. Consistent measurement. Those were requirements for good marketing a decade before AI showed up.

Most Israeli B2B startups skipped them, because they never hired real marketing leadership in the first place.

So AI doesn't fix the missing foundation.

It executes the chaos faster, and at scale.
