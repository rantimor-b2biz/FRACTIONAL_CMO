# Research Briefing Skill — Fractional CMO

Turn any research into a one-page brief you can act on in 2 minutes.

## When to Use

- Competitor or market analysis
- Client strategy session prep
- Industry trend summaries
- Briefing before a keynote or workshop
- Decision support for Ran or a client

---

## The Briefing Structure (Always 5 Sections)

### 1. Executive Summary (2-3 sentences)
The key finding in plain language. If someone reads nothing else, they get the point.

### 2. Key Findings (3-5 bullets)
Each finding = one sentence with a specific number or detail. No fluff.

### 3. Analysis (1-2 paragraphs)
What this means for the business. Connections between findings. Label opinions clearly.

### 4. Recommendations (2-3 actions)
Concrete. Specific. Tied to findings. Not "consider exploring" — actual actions.

### 5. Sources & Limitations
Where data came from. What couldn't be verified. What to watch next.

## Templates by Brief Type

### Competitor Analysis Brief
```
Executive Summary: [Who they are, their positioning, your competitive advantage in 2-3 sentences]

Key Findings:
1. Their positioning: [what they say vs what they do]
2. Their audience: [who they serve, overlap with Ran's ICP]
3. Their content angle: [what topics they own on LinkedIn]
4. Their weakness: [what's missing or thin]
5. Recent moves: [anything new in last 3 months]

Analysis: [What this means for Ran's positioning and content strategy]

Recommendations:
1. [Own this angle because they're not covering it]
2. [Differentiate on this specific point]

Sources: [URLs]
```

### Market Trend Brief
```
Executive Summary: [What's shifting in B2B marketing, why it matters for founders now]

Key Findings:
1. [Trend + data point]
2. [Trend + data point]
3. [Trend + data point]

Analysis: [What founders are thinking/doing as a result]

Recommendations:
1. [Content angle to publish while this is fresh]
2. [Service angle this opens up]

Sources: [URLs + dates]
```

### Client Strategy Brief
```
Executive Summary: [Situation, core challenge, recommended focus]

Key Findings:
1. [What's working in their current marketing]
2. [Where the gap is]
3. [What their ICP actually cares about]
4. [Competitive landscape snapshot]

Analysis: [Root cause of the challenge + why this approach will work]

Recommendations:
1. [Priority 1 action with rationale]
2. [Priority 2 action with rationale]
3. [What NOT to do]

Sources: [Client materials reviewed, research done]
```

## Writing Rules

**Lead with the conclusion:** First sentence = the answer. Don't build to it.

**Numbers over adjectives:**
```
Bad: "The market is growing significantly"
Good: "The market grew 34% YoY, now at $2.1B"
```

**Label certainty:**
```
Fact: "Per Gartner 2026 report..."
Estimated: "Based on team size and pricing, estimated $3-5M revenue"
Signal: "3 job postings in 30 days suggest enterprise expansion"
```

**Recommendations must be specific:**
```
Bad: "Consider refining your positioning"
Good: "Rewrite the LinkedIn headline from [X] to [Y] — it addresses the ICP's specific fear"
```

---

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Executive summary stands alone (full picture in 3 sentences)?
- [ ] At least 3 specific numbers in findings?
- [ ] Facts labeled separately from opinions?
- [ ] Recommendations are actionable (verb + what + why)?
- [ ] Sources listed?
- [ ] Scannable in 2 minutes?

---

*Every line earns its place. If it doesn't inform a decision, cut it.*
