## Research Workflows

### Workflow 1: LinkedIn Content Research

**Goal:** Find data + contrarian angles for a specific LinkedIn topic.

**Steps:**
1. Search: `[topic] statistics 2026` (get real numbers)
2. Search: `[topic] site:linkedin.com` (what's being discussed right now)
3. Search: `[topic] "what nobody talks about"` OR `[topic] myth`
4. Fetch 2-3 top articles for quotes and data
5. Search in Hebrew: `[topic in Hebrew]` for Israeli perspective

**Output:**
```markdown
## Content Research Brief: [Topic]

### Key Numbers
- [Stat + source + date]
- [Stat + source + date]

### What Everyone Is Saying
[The mainstream take — what to disagree with or build on]

### The Contrarian Angle
[What's underexplored or misunderstood]

### Israeli Market Context
[If relevant — what's different/same in Israel]

### Best Hook Ideas
- [Hook 1]
- [Hook 2]
- [Hook 3]
```

---

### Workflow 2: Competitor / Peer Positioning Research

**Goal:** Understand how other Fractional CMOs or B2B consultants position themselves.

**Steps:**
1. Search: `fractional CMO LinkedIn [location/industry]`
2. Fetch their LinkedIn profiles + recent posts
3. Search: `fractional CMO pricing positioning 2026`
4. Note: what angles do they own? What's the gap?

**Output:**
```markdown
## Competitor Brief: [Person/Company]

**Role:** [Title]
**Positioning:** [How they describe themselves]
**Audience:** [Who they serve]
**Content Angle:** [What topics they own]
**Tone:** [Formal/casual/provocative]

### Gap for Ran
[What they're NOT doing that Ran can own]
```

### Workflow 3: Trend Research for Content Calendar

**Goal:** Find what B2B founders are talking about right now.

**Steps:**
1. Search: `B2B marketing trends 2026`
2. Search: `founder challenges 2026 Israel` (and in Hebrew)
3. Scan LinkedIn for trending topics in B2B marketing this week
4. Check Lenny's Newsletter / First Round Review for recent pieces

**Output format:** Update `B-brain/content-opportunities.md` with findings.

## Research Quality Rules

- **Verify before trusting:** One source = claim. Three sources = fact.
- **Date everything:** Always note publication date. Under 6 months = solid.
- **Label clearly:**
  - **Fact:** Verified data with URL
  - **Signal:** Pattern you observed ("3 recent LinkedIn posts by founders show...")
  - **Opinion:** Your interpretation (label it)
- **Search in Hebrew too:** For Israeli market content, Hebrew search often reveals different data.

## Search Tips

| Bad Query | Better Query |
|-----------|-------------|
| "B2B marketing" | "B2B marketing attribution founders 2026" |
| "LinkedIn tips" | "LinkedIn engagement rate B2B posts 2026" |
| "Israeli startups" | "Israeli startup Series A CMO hiring 2026" |

**Useful operators:**
- `site:linkedin.com "[topic]"` — find LinkedIn posts
- `"[topic]" filetype:pdf` — find research reports
- `[topic] after:2025` — recent only
