# Strategic Decision Skill — Fractional CMO

Turn any strategic decision into a one-page brief with a clear recommendation.

## When to Use

- Should Ran offer a new service or package?
- Should a client hire a CMO vs continue fractional?
- Which market or ICP to focus on next?
- Build vs buy a marketing capability?
- Content strategy pivot decision?

---

## Decision Brief Structure (7 Sections)

### 1. Decision Required (1 sentence)
Frame it as a clear choice. Not "should we explore" — "Do we launch X by Q2 or wait?"

### 2. Context (2-3 sentences)
Why now? What triggered this? What's the cost of NOT deciding?

### 3. Options (2-4 options)
For each:
- What it is (1-2 sentences)
- Pros (bullets)
- Cons (bullets)
- Estimated impact (numbers, not adjectives)
- Confidence level (High / Medium / Low)

Always include "Do Nothing" as an option with its real cost.

### 4. Risk Assessment
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|

### 5. Recommendation
Go with [Option]. Confidence: [High/Medium/Low].
Why: [2-3 sentences integrating both optimistic and cautious views]
Key condition: [What must be true for this to work]

Take a stand. "It depends" is not a recommendation.

### 6. What We Don't Know
1. [Uncertainty] — How to resolve: [action]

### 7. Next Steps
1. [Action] — [Who] — [By when]

## Decision Templates for This Client

### New Service / Offer Decision
```
Decision: Launch [service] by [date] or wait?
Key factors: Market demand evidence, time cost, pricing model, ICP fit
Common trap: Building what's interesting vs what solves a proven problem
Success metric: [X paying clients in Y months]
```

### ICP Focus Decision
```
Decision: Double down on [ICP A] or expand to [ICP B]?
Key factors: Current win rate, deal size, referral patterns, competition density
Common trap: Chasing bigger market at the cost of the niche where you win
```

### Content Strategy Pivot
```
Decision: Continue [current format] or shift to [new format]?
Key factors: Engagement trend, audience growth rate, effort cost, differentiation
Common trap: Pivoting too early (3 months isn't enough signal)
```

### Client Engagement Decision (for Ran's clients)
```
Decision: Does [client] need [service A] or [service B] right now?
Key factors: Current pain, budget, timeline, internal capacity
Common trap: Solving the stated problem, not the root problem
```

---

## Writing Rules

**Frame before analyzing:**
Bad: "Here are some options..."
Good: "The decision: Do we launch X this quarter or Q3? We need to decide now because [reason]."

**Numbers over adjectives:**
Bad: "Significant revenue opportunity"
Good: "Estimated 3-5 new clients at $X/month = $Y ARR"

**Make trade-offs explicit:**
Bad: "Option A is better"
Good: "Option A is 3x faster but 2x the cost. Option B is cheaper but we lose 2 months."

**Recommend — don't hedge:**
Bad: "It depends on various factors"
Good: "Go with Option A. Confidence: Medium. Main risk: [specific thing]."

---

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Decision framed in one clear sentence?
- [ ] All options have numbers (even estimates)?
- [ ] "Do Nothing" included with its cost?
- [ ] Clear recommendation with confidence level?
- [ ] Next steps specific (who, what, when)?
- [ ] Scannable in 2 minutes?

---

*A decision brief isn't about having all the answers. It's about having enough clarity to act.*
