# Social Post Skill — Fractional CMO LinkedIn

Write LinkedIn posts that Israeli founders stop to read. One sharp insight. Direct voice. No fluff.

## Client Context

**Platform:** LinkedIn (only social channel for this client)
**Persona:** Israeli founder, B2B CEO, growth-stage business leader
**Voice:** Ran Timor — executive, direct, slightly contrarian, experience-based
**Goal:** Build thought leadership positioning, drive qualified inbound conversations
**See also:** `C-core/voice-dna.md`, `T-tools/skills/linkedin-copywriting-skill.md` (template library)

---

## Post Length

- **Standard LinkedIn post:** 200-350 words
- **Hook-heavy short post:** 100-150 words (punchy contrarian takes)
- **Framework post:** 300-400 words (when listing 3+ items)
- **Never:** Over 450 words without strong justification

---

## Hook Formulas (Ran's Voice)

### Hook Type 1: Founder Situation
Opens with a situation the reader immediately recognizes.

```
Your pitch deck is clear.
Your website is not.

That disconnect is costing you enterprise deals.
```

Works because: The reader nods. They've been there.

---

### Hook Type 2: The Counterintuitive
Challenges the thing founders assume is true.

```
Positioning beats performance marketing.

Not sometimes. Almost always.
```

Works because: Short, definitive, invites disagreement or agreement — both drive engagement.

---

### Hook Type 3: Specific Number or Observation
Opens with precision — signals this is not vague advice.

```
3 of the last 5 founders I spoke to had the same problem.

They could explain their product clearly. They could not explain why it mattered.
```

Works because: Specific numbers feel real. "Most founders" is forgettable. "3 of the last 5" is not.

---

### Hook Type 4: The Confession
Ran admitting something — creates authenticity and immediate trust.

```
I was wrong about positioning for years.

I thought it was a messaging problem. It's a strategic problem.
```

Works because: Executives who admit mistakes are rare. This earns respect immediately.

---

### Hook Type 5: The Provocation
A statement that makes the reader slightly uncomfortable.

```
Hiring a CMO before you have a position is expensive confusion.
```

Works because: The founder either pushes back mentally (engagement) or recognizes themselves (saves).

## Post Structures

### Structure 1: Insight Post (Most Common — 40% of posts)

```
[HOOK: 1-2 lines that stop the scroll]

[WHY IT IS TRUE: 2-3 sentences explaining the insight, from experience]

[WHAT MOST PEOPLE DO INSTEAD: the wrong approach, briefly]

[WHAT TO DO: 3-4 bullet points or 2-3 sentences]

[CLOSING LINE: one sharp, memorable sentence]
```

**Voice check:** Would a founder read this and think "this person has actually done this"?

---

### Structure 2: Contrarian Post

```
[BOLD CONTRARIAN STATEMENT: 1 line]

[EXPECTED ARGUMENT: what people usually say]

[WHY THAT IS WRONG: specific, experience-based, not theoretical]

[WHAT ACTUALLY WORKS: the alternative, briefly but concretely]

[CLOSING QUESTION OR STATEMENT: invites a response]
```

---

### Structure 3: Founder Story (Without Naming the Client)

```
[SITUATION HOOK: "Last month a founder called me..." or "I've seen this three times now..."]

[THE PROBLEM: what was happening — specific enough to feel real]

[WHAT WE DISCOVERED: the insight underneath the surface problem]

[WHAT CHANGED: the outcome of addressing the real issue]

[THE LESSON: one transferable takeaway for the reader]
```

**Note:** Never identify clients. Anonymize industry, company size, and specifics.

---

### Structure 4: Framework Post

```
[HOOK: name the framework, make it sound like the reader needs it]

[2-4 ITEMS: each one short — one insight per bullet, not a paragraph]

[THE PRINCIPLE THAT CONNECTS THEM: what all the items share]

[CTA or CLOSING: question, statement, or invitation to share]
```

## Ran's Voice Checklist (Run Before Every Post)

- [ ] Short sentences? (Max 15 words per sentence is a good test)
- [ ] ZERO em dashes (—)? This is a hard rule. Replace with periods, commas, or colons.
- [ ] No buzzwords? (No: synergy, disruptive, game-changer, leverage, optimize, revolutionize)
- [ ] Specific enough? ("some founders" → how many; "a lot" → rough number)
- [ ] Experience-based? Does it sound like someone who has done this, not read about it?
- [ ] Israeli founder angle? Is there a local market dimension or is it universal?
- [ ] One main idea? Not three squeezed into one post.
- [ ] Mobile-readable? Short paragraphs, breathing room between ideas.
- [ ] No exclamation marks? Almost never appropriate for this voice.
- [ ] Engagement question at end (optional)? A real question, not a soft "Thoughts?"

## Hashtags

3-5 at end of post, after a line break:

**Standard set:**
```
#B2BMarketing #Founders #GTMStrategy #MarketingStrategy #IsraeliStartups
```

**Vary based on content:**
- Positioning content: `#Positioning #ProductMarketing`
- AI content: `#AIStrategy #AIMarketing`
- Leadership content: `#Leadership #CEOInsights`

**No external links in the caption.** If there is a link (article, resource), put it in the first comment. External links reduce LinkedIn reach significantly.

## What to Avoid

- Long intros that warm up before the insight (cut them)
- Hedging: "It might be that...", "Some would say...", "Perhaps..."
- Aspirational phrasing: "Unlock your potential", "Transform your business"
- Passive voice: "It was discovered that..." → "I discovered that..."
- Vague CTAs: "Let me know what you think" → Ask a real question or make a clear point
- Starting with "I" (LinkedIn often penalizes this in algorithm) — start with the idea, not the person
