# Weekly Review — Fractional CMO

Close the loop. What worked, what didn't, what to do differently.

## When to Run

End of every week or content cycle. Takes 15 minutes. Makes the system smarter.

---

## The Review (5 Questions)

### 1. What content did we publish this week?
List each piece: format, topic, channel.

### 2. What worked?
For each piece, check:
- LinkedIn: impressions, likes, comments, profile visits, DMs
- Newsletter: open rate, click rate, replies
- Blog: views, shares

Flag the clear winner. What specifically made it work? (Hook? Topic? Format? Timing?)

### 3. What didn't work?
Flag the underperformer. Be honest. Was it:
- The hook?
- The topic (not relevant to audience)?
- The format?
- The timing?
- The length?

### 4. What pattern are we seeing?
After 3+ data points:
- Which topics generate the most comments?
- Which hook types get the most engagement?
- What does Ran's audience actually respond to vs what we think they respond to?

### 5. What do we do differently next week?
One specific change. Not "post more." Something concrete.

---

## The Loop Closing

After answering the 5 questions, update:

**If a pattern appeared 3+ times:**
→ Promote to `C-core/voice-dna.md` (under "What Works" section)

**If audience language was noted:**
→ Add to `C-core/icp-profile.md` (under "Language They Use")

**If a content type underperformed consistently:**
→ Log in `M-memory/decisions.md` as a decision to pause/change it

**Always:**
→ Update `M-memory/learning-log.md` with the week's key finding
