# Create Hook Bank — Fractional CMO

Build a library of proven opening lines for Ran's LinkedIn content.

## When to Use

- Copywriter is writing a new post and needs hook options
- Monthly: refresh the hook bank with new patterns
- When a hook dramatically outperformed — capture it

---

## What Is a Hook Bank?

A library of first-line formulas that have worked for THIS audience (founders and B2B leaders). Not generic LinkedIn advice. Ran's specific patterns.

---

## How to Build It

### Step 1: Audit Past Hits

Look at Ran's top 10 performing LinkedIn posts. For each:
- What was the first line?
- What TYPE of hook was it? (see types below)
- What made it work?

### Step 2: Identify the Patterns

Group the first lines by type:

**Type A: The Founder Situation**
```
"A founder called me last week. His pipeline had dried up."
"I just finished a 2-hour strategy session with a Series B CEO."
"Three founders asked me the same question this month."
```
Works because: Immediately signals relevance to the ICP.

**Type B: The Counterintuitive Opening**
```
"More marketing won't fix your problem."
"Your pipeline isn't a sales problem."
"The CMO hire won't solve this."
```
Works because: Forces a founder to stop and reconsider.

**Type C: The Specific Number**
```
"I've reviewed 40 startup marketing strategies."
"After 12 years of B2B marketing, here's what I know for certain."
"75% of the founders I talk to make the same mistake."
```
Works because: Specificity = credibility.

**Type D: The Confession / Honest Moment**
```
"I used to give the wrong advice on this."
"I made this mistake twice before I understood it."
"I almost told a founder to do the wrong thing last week."
```
Works because: Vulnerability signals authenticity.

**Type E: The Provocation**
```
"Stop posting on LinkedIn. It won't help."
"The worst thing that can happen to your startup is viral content."
"Most marketing advice for startups is written by people who've never run marketing for a startup."
```
Works because: Creates immediate reaction (agree or disagree — both engage).

### Step 3: Write 5 New Hooks Per Type

For each type above, write 5 original hooks that haven't been used yet. Use Ran's voice.

## Usage Rules

- Copywriter checks hook bank before writing any post
- After a hook WORKS well: log it in `M-memory/learning-log.md` AND add a variant here
- After a hook underperforms: note it in the bank with [LOW PERFORMANCE] tag
- Refresh the bank monthly (add 5, retire 2-3 overused ones)
