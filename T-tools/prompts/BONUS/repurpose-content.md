# Repurpose Content — Fractional CMO

Turn one strong piece of content into 5 formats.

## When to Use

You have a LinkedIn post, article, or newsletter that performed well. Now extract maximum value from it.

---

## The Repurposing Map

One strong piece becomes:

| From | Into | Effort |
|------|------|--------|
| LinkedIn post | Newsletter section | Low |
| Newsletter | 3x LinkedIn posts | Low |
| Blog post / article | 5x LinkedIn posts + newsletter | Medium |
| Client case study | Blog post + LinkedIn story + newsletter | Medium |
| Workshop / talk | Blog post + post series + newsletter | High |

---

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Identify the Core Insight
What is the ONE thing this content says that nobody else is saying exactly that way?

Write it in one sentence: "The insight is: ___"

### Step 2: Extract the Pieces
From the original, find:
- [ ] The hook / opening situation
- [ ] The key data point or number
- [ ] The counterintuitive claim
- [ ] The framework (if any)
- [ ] The closing line / provocation

### Step 3: Rebuild for Each Format

**LinkedIn Post (from newsletter/blog):**
- Take the hook
- Pick ONE insight from the middle
- Add a direct question at the end
- Max 200 words

**Newsletter Section (from LinkedIn post):**
- Expand the context (2-3 more sentences explaining why)
- Add one example or case (real or anonymized)
- Add an application step for the reader

**Carousel / List Post (from framework):**
- Title: "The [X] things founders get wrong about [topic]"
- Each slide/line = one item from the framework
- Last slide = the sharp takeaway

**Story Post (from case study):**
- Open with the moment the founder was stuck
- Skip to the insight (not every detail)
- End with: "Here's what I would tell them now"

---

## Quality Checks

- Does each repurposed piece work STANDALONE (no "as I mentioned before")?
- Is the voice still Ran's? (Direct, specific, experience-based)
- Is each format right for its channel?
- No em dashes?
