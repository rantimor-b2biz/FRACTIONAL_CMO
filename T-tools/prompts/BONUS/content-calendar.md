- When the week feels scattered or unplanned
- Before a strategic campaign or push
- After reviewing performance and needing a fresh roadmap

## Calendar Building Process

### Step 1: Check the Quarterly Theme

What month/theme are we in? From `B-brain/content-calendar.md`:
- What is the macro theme for this period?
- What 2-3 topics are on deck?
- Any events or news hooks to connect to?

### Step 2: Select Topics

For each week, choose from:
- **Positioning / clarity** — Core Ran territory
- **GTM strategy** — Founder go-to-market challenges
- **Marketing execution** — What CMO-level thinking looks like in practice
- **AI / tools** — How Israeli founders use AI strategically (not tactically)
- **Contrarian take** — Challenge a piece of conventional wisdom
- **Story** — Real founder situation (without naming the client)

**Mix rule:** Max 1 promotional per week. Min 1 contrarian or story per week.

### Step 3: Assign Hook Types

For each post, assign one of Ran's hook types:
- **Specific number** — "3 of the last 5 founders I've worked with made the same mistake"
- **Confession** — "I was wrong about positioning for years"
- **Contrarian opener** — "Your marketing isn't the problem"
- **Founder situation** — "Your pitch deck is clear. Your website is not."
- **Provocation** — "Hiring a CMO before you have a position is expensive confusion"

### Step 4: Map to Calendar Table

## Rules

- **No more than 1 promotional post per 5 posts** — Ran's audience tolerates, not enjoys, promos
- **Israeli context in at least 1 post per week** — Not forced, but present
- **Every post needs a hook, not just a topic** — Topic = "positioning." Hook = "Your pitch is clear. Your website is not."
- **Check M-memory before finalizing** — Don't repeat a topic from the last 3 weeks
- **Leave 1-2 slots open per week for timely news hooks** — The best posts often react to the week
