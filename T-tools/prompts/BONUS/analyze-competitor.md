# Analyze Competitor — Fractional CMO

Map how a competitor or peer positions themselves, own the gap.

## When to Use

- Researching another fractional CMO or B2B marketing consultant
- Understanding what ICP overlap exists with Ran
- Finding content angles that are unoccupied
- Preparing a client for a competitive landscape session

---

## Research Process

### Step 1: Find Their Digital Footprint
- LinkedIn profile: headline, about section, recent posts
- Website: homepage, services page, about page
- Any podcast appearances, articles, talks

### Step 2: Answer 6 Questions

**1. What is their positioning claim?**
(How do they describe themselves in 1-2 sentences?)

**2. Who is their ICP?**
(Stage of company, industry, role they target)

**3. What content topics do they own?**
(What do they post about consistently on LinkedIn?)

**4. What is their engagement level?**
(Average likes/comments per post — rough signal of audience size and resonance)

**5. What is their business model?**
(Retainer? Project? Productized service? Course?)

**6. What is their tone?**
(Academic? Practical? Provocative? Inspirational?)
